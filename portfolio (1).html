Superstore Sales & Product Performance Dashboard

$2.30MTotal Sales
$286.40KTotal Profit
12.47%Profit Margin
46.88%YoY Growth

Problem

Which categories and products really make money, not just sell the most, and how fast is the business growing?

Approach

Modeled the Orders and date tables, wrote DAX measures for profit margin %, YoY growth, and running total sales, and built two report pages: an Executive Summary and a Product Analysis page with segment, year, ship mode, and category slicers.

Key insights

  • Technology leads with $836K sales and $145K profit at a 17.40% margin.
  • Office Supplies earns a 17.04% margin on $719K sales.
  • Furniture sells $742K but earns only a 2.49% margin, the weakest category.
  • Profit drops toward zero and below as discounts rise.
  • Running total sales climb steadily from 2016 to 2019.

Business impact

Shows where profit comes from and where sales fail to turn into profit, so pricing and discount decisions can focus on Furniture and high-discount orders.

Sales vs Budget Analysis Dashboard

$22.99MSales
$22.86MTarget
100.6%Achievement
+$131.24KVariance

Problem

Are actual sales meeting targets, and which months and products are ahead or behind?

Approach

Built calendar and product dimension tables, then measures for achievement %, variance, and variance %. Visuals include a monthly combo chart with a variance line, a product matrix with conditional formatting, a sales share pie, and an achievement gauge.

Key insights

  • Total sales of $22.99M beat the $22.86M target by $131,242 (0.6%).
  • Carlota reached 102.2% of target (+$168,765).
  • Quad reached 101.1% (+$69,950).
  • Aspen missed at 98.8% (-$107,473).
  • Most sales and targets are concentrated in October to December.

Business impact

Gives managers a fast read on target performance and points to the product that needs attention (Aspen), so gaps can be addressed early.

Sales Analysis Dashboard

RevenueKPI
QuantityKPI
OrdersKPI

Problem

How do revenue, quantity, and orders vary across products, sales reps, regions, segments, and time?

Approach

Built PivotTables from the transaction data and linked them to PivotCharts on a single dashboard sheet, with dynamic slicers for year, quarter, month, product, and segment.

Key insights

  • The top 7 sales reps sit in a tight revenue band, from $22.26M to $22.92M.
  • Average product price ranges from $20 to $28 across the five products.
  • Quarterly revenue moves in a narrow range across 2014 to 2016.
  • Order segments are split between HVO and LVO orders.

Business impact

Lets managers filter in one click to find top performers, strong regions, and revenue patterns, without rebuilding reports.

Customer Segmentation Analysis Dashboard

$725KTop region (West)
$392KLowest region (South)

Problem

Who are the most valuable customers and regions across segments, categories, and locations?

Approach

Built PivotTables and PivotCharts with slicers for order year, city, state, category, segment, and region so any slice of customers can be explored.

Key insights

  • West is the top region at $725K, followed by East ($679K) and Central ($501K).
  • South is the lowest at $392K.
  • Sales trend upward from 2016 through 2019.
  • Segments: Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office.

Business impact

Shows which customer groups and regions deserve the most sales focus, and where growth opportunities remain (South).
